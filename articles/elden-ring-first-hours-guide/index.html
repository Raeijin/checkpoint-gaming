ELDEN RING GUIDE

Elden Ring Beginner Guide: What to Do in Your First Few Hours

A spoiler-light route through the early game: exploration, upgrades, map fragments, summons and when to stop headbutting a boss.

Checked 20 August 2026•9 MIN•Research-backed
ON THIS PAGE Quick answer Key habits Main guide What to buy / use Related articles
Quick answer

Explore Limgrave before forcing the main route. Unlock Sites of Grace, collect map fragments, upgrade your main weapon, spend runes before risky fights, and use Spirit Ashes when the game allows them.

DO THIS
  • Explore before obsessing over the main path
  • Spend runes before risky experiments
  • Use map markers and return to hard fights later
AVOID THIS
  • Repeatedly bash into one boss because the game points at it
  • Spread early stats across everything
  • Treat every death as lost progress rather than information
AT A GLANCE

Key facts

Do not treat the golden path as a command

The guidance from Sites of Grace points toward major objectives, but Elden Ring expects you to wander. If an early boss feels wildly stronger than you, exploring elsewhere is usually the intended answer rather than repeating the same fight for hours.

Prioritise weapon upgrades

A weapon you understand and have upgraded is more valuable than constantly swapping to every new weapon you find. Smithing Stones are worth hunting early because weapon level has a large effect on practical damage.

Buy the crafting kit and use map markers

Early merchants sell useful basics, and the map lets you place your own markers. Mark caves, locked areas and bosses you want to revisit instead of relying on memory.

Spend runes before dangerous exploration

If you are carrying enough runes for a level, spending them before entering a new dungeon removes a lot of unnecessary stress.

Use the tools the game gives you

Spirit Ashes, guard counters, jumping attacks, stealth and mounted combat are core mechanics rather than emergency cheats. Different encounters reward different tools.

Build one reliable damage plan

Early on, pick a weapon moveset you understand and meet its requirements instead of spreading stats across every interesting item you find. Vigor is rarely a wasted early investment because surviving one more hit creates more opportunities to learn.

Use the world as your difficulty slider

If a boss feels absurd, leave. Explore caves, collect upgrade materials, unlock map fragments and come back stronger. Elden Ring's open structure is designed to let preparation replace repeated frustration.

Use consumables and summons

Items, Spirit Ashes and crafted tools are part of the game, not an admission of defeat. Experiment early so you understand what is available before a difficult encounter forces you to learn under pressure.

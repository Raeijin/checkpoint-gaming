MOZA R5 vs Logitech G923: the short answer

On PC, the R5 is the stronger hardware platform. Direct drive removes the gears between motor and wheel, the 5.5 Nm output gives more useful force-feedback headroom, and MOZA's detachable-wheel ecosystem is built for expansion. The G923 wins when ease of console compatibility and a clutch pedal in the box matter more.

Is the Logitech G923 direct drive?

No. The G923 is gear-driven. TRUEFORCE is a feedback technology, not a direct-drive motor design.

Logitech rates the G923 at 2.3 Nm with a dual-motor force-feedback system. TRUEFORCE can add higher-frequency game information in supported titles, but the wheel still transmits force through helical gears. The R5 drives the wheel directly from the motor shaft.

How different does the torque look on paper?

The R5 is rated at 5.5 Nm while Logitech lists 2.3 Nm for the G923. Torque is not the only measure of feedback quality, but that gap is large enough to represent a genuinely different hardware class rather than a minor spec bump.

Console support can reverse the recommendation

The standard R5 bundle is aimed at PC. Logitech sells G923 versions for Xbox/PC and PlayStation/PC. If your main platform is a console and you want minimum setup friction, the G923 can be the more sensible purchase even though the R5 is stronger hardware on PC.

Pedals: Logitech gives you a clutch immediately

The G923 bundle includes accelerator, brake and clutch pedals. The standard R5 bundle starts with throttle and brake. If you drive manual road cars and want a clutch immediately, include the extra MOZA pedal cost when comparing complete prices.

Upgrade path

The R5's strongest advantage after force feedback is ecosystem expansion. You can change wheels and move into better MOZA pedals, shifters and handbrakes. The G923 is much more of a complete fixed starter system.

Who should buy the G923?

Someone who finds it at a strong sale price, wants a complete three-pedal package, or values console support more than direct-drive performance. It is not obsolete; it is simply competing against much stronger entry-level hardware than it did at launch.

Who should buy the R5?

A PC racer who expects to keep upgrading. If you know you are likely to buy a wheel stand, better pedals or different wheel rims later, the R5 gives that spending somewhere to go.

Compare the complete checkout cost

Put the wheel, pedals, any clutch or shifter you need, mounting and delivery in each basket. A cheaper headline bundle can stop being cheaper once those extras are included. Check the exact platform: current G923 listings also include a PC-only option, so the name alone does not establish console compatibility.

Should an existing G923 owner upgrade?

If you are happy with steering but struggle to brake consistently, first stop your chair and pedals moving. If you want a detachable wheel and direct drive, compare the R5 with the newer R5 Pro before buying. COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is the G923 direct drive?

No. Logitech specifies dual-motor force feedback with helical gearing. TRUEFORCE does not make it direct drive.

Should I buy the R5 or G923 for console?

The standard R5 bundle is for PC. For a G923, choose the variant licensed for your console; a PC-only listing is not interchangeable.

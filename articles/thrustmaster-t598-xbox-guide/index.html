Thrustmaster T598 Xbox Guide: Compatibility, Versions and What to Buy

There is now an Xbox/PC T598, but it is not the same licensed version as the PlayStation model. Here is what Xbox buyers need to check before ordering.

Checked 27 August 2026•15 MIN•Research-backed

Quick answer

Yes, the Thrustmaster T598 is available in an Xbox/PC version for Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One and Windows PC. It uses the same 5 Nm Direct Axial Drive concept, Race Dash and two-pedal Raceline LTE bundle format. The important catch is version selection: the Xbox/PC and PlayStation/PC T598 are separate licensed products.

BUY IF
  • You want entry direct drive on Xbox Series X|S or Xbox One
  • You also want to use the same wheel on PC
  • You like the Thrustmaster detachable-wheel ecosystem and Race Dash
SKIP IF
  • You need PlayStation support — buy the PlayStation/PC T598 instead
  • You already own a PC-only direct-drive setup that suits you
  • You are not willing to update firmware or check game-specific compatibility modes

Is the T598 available for Xbox?

Yes. Thrustmaster now lists a dedicated T598 Xbox / PC model compatible with Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One and Windows PC. This is separate from the T598 PlayStation / PC version.

Do not accidentally buy the PlayStation version

The two products look closely related and share the same T598 name, but platform licensing differs. For an Xbox setup, the listing should explicitly say Xbox / PC. For PS5 or PS4, you want the PlayStation / PC version.

What is in the Xbox bundle?

Thrustmaster lists the T598 servo base, detachable Sportcar wheel, Raceline Pedals LTE two-pedal set, clamping system, power supply, USB cable and pedal cable in the complete Xbox/PC package.

Force feedback

The Xbox model uses Thrustmaster's Direct Axial Drive system with 5 Nm of constant torque. Thrustmaster also advertises overshoot capability for short boosted effects and high-frequency HARMONY feedback.

Native and Legacy modes on Xbox

This is the detail most quick product pages miss. Thrustmaster's support documentation lists two Xbox compatibility modes: Native is the default; Legacy makes the T598 identify as a TS-XW Racer for backwards compatibility.

Thrustmaster specifically notes that some titles, including recent F1 games, can require Legacy mode. If a game sees the wheel but behaves incorrectly, checking the support page and compatibility mode should be one of the first troubleshooting steps.

Firmware matters

Thrustmaster's current support page lists wheelbase firmware 1.60 as the latest version at our last check. Firmware is updated through My Thrustmaster Panel on PC. If you buy the wheel for Xbox only, it is still worth connecting it to a PC for firmware maintenance when required.

One small Xbox limitation

Thrustmaster notes that the LSB and RSB action buttons on the base are functional on PC but not on Xbox. That is not a reason to reject the wheel, but it is exactly the sort of compatibility detail worth knowing before purchase.

T598 Xbox vs MOZA R5

For Xbox, the T598 has the decisive advantage because the standard MOZA R5 is PC-focused. If you are choosing on PC, the comparison becomes much closer — see our dedicated MOZA R5 vs Thrustmaster T598 comparison.

Can you buy the T598 through Amazon?

Yes, when Amazon UK has the relevant version listed. Because the Xbox and PlayStation models can appear in the same search results, use the Xbox-specific check below and confirm the product title before checkout.

Research, not invented experience

Some pages are researched reviews or comparisons based on manufacturer specifications, primary sources and product positioning. Those pages do not pretend the product was physically tested. If hands-on testing is ever used, it should be stated explicitly.

AI-assisted, human-directed

AI tools may assist with research organisation, drafting, editing, image creation and site production. Important product claims are checked against source material, and AI assistance is not treated as a substitute for evidence.
